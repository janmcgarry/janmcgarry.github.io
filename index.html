Jan McGarry Furriol

I am a PhD student at Stockholm University, under the supervision of Alexander Berglund. I previously studied at the University of Barcelona and at the University of Copenhagen. My main research interests are in algebraic topology and homotopy theory. I study manifold bundles and their characteristic classes, mapping class groups, and moduli spaces in topology more generally.
